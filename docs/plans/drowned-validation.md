# Drowned mechanic foundation

Three connected rooms extend the lower Lookout exit: Service Basin, Dry Pump House and Float Chamber. Existing exploration saves are accepted without replacing the profile. Permanent flags retain the recovered impeller, pump repair, upper return and Air Jump. The map includes the new loop.

The manual bleed works before the impeller is available. High, middle and low marks are visible. Static water returns the player to dry ground and resets temporary water low; it does not delete recovered parts. The float valve is on a dry stair that remains safe at high water. The player can wait for the float to settle before boarding. Returning to a repaired basin leaves its main street dry. Air Jump is optional for this district's required route and becomes available after latching its return.

State tests cover missing-part rejection, water settings, recovery gates, permanent rewards, save/reload, hazard reset and the dry high-water control. Controller-route tests cover draining, part recovery, both basin escape stairs, pump climb, float boarding, upper landing and protocol recovery without Dash or Air Jump. Initial traversal failures exposed a low ceiling and excessive boarding gap; both were corrected in the room layout.

Rendered captures were inspected for collision/surface readability and water bounds. Drowned uses subdued civic facades, green-gray water and bright surface markers; colour is not needed to identify the water edge. Restoration relights the civic windows. Art is a procedural foundation pending the reviewed PixelLab kit, atmospheric/audio polish and broader playtesting.

The full game is not complete. Stand, the remaining Field/Flats content, Wire, Array, Approach and Gate, soundtrack integration, accessibility controls, performance checks and release export remain in the campaign plan.
