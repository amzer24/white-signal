## Campaign direction

Use an original recurring short question-and-answer motif across districts. Listening Field is cold, angular and suspicious, with clipped pulse machinery. Drowned Array uses hollow low pulses and unsettled slow arpeggios; drainage exposes a harder, more articulated rhythm. The Stand adds sharp rhythmic pressure during local conductor trials while exploration retains deliberate gaps. Restoration variations should improve rhythmic coherence while keeping the dark identity, and should use compatible tempo and phrase grids only after the base theme is selected.

Keep music, weather, mechanisms and actionable warnings on separate buses. Do not bake thunder or danger warnings into music. Short one-shot SFX should be reviewed individually for precise onset, useful duration and lack of musical tails. Existing user-created Suno rain, thunder and 8-bit lightning clips were observed but not modified, exported or assessed in this task.
