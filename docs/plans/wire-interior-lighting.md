# Wire shaft interior and relighting

An opaque interior layer replaces the generic outdoor backdrop in the maintenance shaft: recessed lift channel, structural panels, overhead control cable and four abandoned terminal bays. Outdoor parallax and dish silhouettes are skipped for this room. All detail sits behind solid geometry, player and controls.

A low cool entry light remains available before repair. Three upper fixtures brighten with the existing saved-release animation; the overhead cable and lamps change state together. Cached coherence-light textures supply steady, quantized falloff. The platform/player palette does not darken and no hazard is hidden by the effect. This is decorative relighting; no light/shadow collision or occlusion system is claimed.

NVIDIA before/after campaign captures inspected at the 480x270 logical viewport. The climb opening, lever, lift and return edge remain visible. Physics, save rules and controls are unchanged. Broader Wire exterior art, interior texture detailing, animations, authored darkness challenges and light occlusion remain future work. The downloadable Wire package predates this change.
