# Dead Carrier loop audition

Status: provisionally integrated into exploration for development review. Tone feedback requested; full listening and gameplay mix review remain open.

## Reproducible local analysis

Source is the unchanged 90-second WAV identified in music-brief.md. Script: `white-signal-team/analyse_music.py`, using the bundled Python runtime and NumPy. No audio or game source was sent to a worker.

Positive spectral flux autocorrelation found a strong candidate near 118 BPM. A longer-lag search over 116–120 BPM estimated 118.30 BPM. This is a periodicity estimate, not proof of exact tempo, meter or downbeat placement. The top 128-beat candidate compares four-beat contexts: chroma similarity 0.883 and onset correlation 0.915. Full numerical report: `prototypes/audio/dead-carrier-analysis.json`.

## Edit

Original sample range [400126, 3516272) at 48000 Hz: 64.919708 seconds. The first 960 samples blend the source continuation after the endpoint into the loop start with a linear ramp. This preserves duration, rather than subtracting the crossfade from the beat cycle. Original remains unchanged.

Maximum stereo wrap sample difference falls from 0.30295 to 0.03101 on a normalized scale. Peak remains 0.59732. These describe numerical continuity; they do not establish an inaudible seam, musical compatibility, or lack of vocals.

## Free-worker review

Accepted separation of numerical checks from listening judgment and exact sample-offset records. Rejected its incorrect statement that 2.03 seconds is four bars at 118 BPM (it is approximately four beats), its claim that low spectral variance establishes repeated phrases, and fixed-target BPM as a substitute for measurement. A linear rather than equal-power blend avoids an unnecessary correlated-signal gain bump.

## Remaining acceptance

Listen to the complete loop and multiple wraps, establish whether the selected grid location is a sensible phrase boundary, assess vocals/timbres and repetition fatigue, then test against footsteps, levers, discharge warnings and thunder at several music volumes. Do not claim a finished soundtrack from the metrics alone.
