# Design artifact validation

The atlas is a design aid, not a game build. Validation below concerns document links, graph consistency and browser interaction. It does not certify physical traversal, puzzle escape or shader performance.

- Eight unique district IDs and 55 unique room IDs within their districts; every edge refers to valid rooms.
- Every district's room graph is connected when its requirements are satisfied.
- Opening-world access includes Flats, Listening Field and the Drowned service lead.
- All 128 combinations of the seven world flags were checked against the four-project Gate requirement.
- The reverse Listening return cannot expose Dash before the west ear is restored. Completing the west ear opens the amplifier and the return controls.
- Drowned freight access requires the upper-conduit completion, matching the puzzle's completion event.
- Atlas data is embedded for offline use. Local document and data links resolve.
- In-browser checks exercised all eight district selections, world-state controls, a topology alternative and a puzzle step. Sixteen block entries rendered; the checked browser session reported no JavaScript errors.
- Visual inspection covered the narrow layout and Listening Field's room graph. Large maps intentionally allow horizontal scrolling where labels would otherwise become unreadable.

The independent design review found and corrected a reverse-route bypass to Dash, conflicting Drowned completion triggers and an ambiguous water reset. A further puzzle review made the workshop service stair deploy with the catch, so it cannot bypass the initial lift lesson and remains usable after power is diverted.

## Pending evidence

No new Godot levels, shaders, scenes or saves were installed by this research. Permanent abilities, inter-district backtracking, ladders, traps and proposed effects remain design targets. The existing Afterlight background assets still need campaign integration.

Required next checks are the real controller's minimum-tool reachability, unusual arrival order, all puzzle states after death and reload, camera framing in vertical rooms, muted/grayscale/reduced-effect cue parity, actual renderer performance, and participant navigation/story comprehension. Developer-account research is complete for this study; annotated commercial room-map/playthrough comparisons remain additional reference work.

The atlas generator, underlying JSON and validation script are included so the design can be revised without losing its explicit constraints.
